# CoinXP Motion Lab

Use the Controls panel to inspect each data-driven visual across its supported states, quality tiers, and motion modes. The Accessibility panel checks the story markup and the stories retain visible labels when motion is reduced or off.

Flame, medallion, and calendar stories use deterministic sample data. They do not call the API or change account progress.
